Cybersecurity Awareness

Learn the basic steps you can take to stay safer online and protect your personal information.

Password Security

Use strong and unique passwords for your online accounts. A strong password should be long and difficult for others to guess.

  • Use at least 8 characters.
  • Use a combination of uppercase and lowercase letters.
  • Include numbers and special characters.
  • Avoid using easily guessed information such as your name or birthday.
  • Do not reuse the same password for every account.

Phishing Awareness

Phishing is a type of cyberattack where attackers try to trick people into revealing sensitive information such as passwords or financial details.

How to Identify Phishing Attempts

  • Be careful with unexpected emails or messages.
  • Check the sender's email address carefully.
  • Do not click suspicious links or download unknown attachments.
  • Be cautious of messages that create a sense of urgency.
  • Verify requests for sensitive information before responding.

Safe Browsing

Safe browsing habits can help reduce the risk of malware, scams, and unauthorized access to your information.

  • Keep your browser and operating system updated.
  • Download software only from trusted sources.
  • Check website addresses before entering sensitive information.
  • Avoid entering personal information on suspicious websites.
  • Be careful when using public or unsecured Wi-Fi networks.

Device Security

Protect your computers and mobile devices by keeping them updated and using appropriate security measures.

  • Keep your operating system and applications updated.
  • Use a screen lock or password on your devices.
  • Install applications from trusted sources.
  • Back up important files regularly.
  • Do not leave your devices unattended in public places.
